\section*{Highlights}
\doublespacing

\begin{itemize}
  \item Support for small modular reactors (SMRs) in the United Kingdom is generally high, but support is lower in Scotland.
  \item Framing SMRs as reactors (as opposed to power stations) in a survey-based experiment decreases support only marginally.
  \item Framing SMRs as being in one's local area (as opposed to the UK) lowers support by only a small amount.
  \item The negative effect of a local area frame is larger in Scotland. Lower support in Scotland is concentrated among supporters of Scottish independence.
  \item Support for SMRs is also lower in those parts of the UK near existing nuclear site.
\end{itemize}
